PhysPosterior: Joint Physical-Observation Posteriors for Real-to-Sim-to-Real

Robots that try something once should know how far to trust the next action. PhysPosterior infers a distribution over physical worlds from a short interaction and runs each of them, so disagreement on a new action is visible before execution.

Abstract

Real-to-sim pipelines usually return a single reconstructed scene. A short interaction, however, rarely pins down friction, mass, or contact, and small errors in the camera or the clock can look like physics. PhysPosterior keeps this ambiguity instead of collapsing it. It infers a joint posterior over physical parameters and observation errors, compiles every sample into an executable simulation world, and runs a new action in all of them. Where the worlds disagree, the risk is visible before the robot acts, and the same belief also produces physics-conditioned training data.

Method

  1. Infer. From limited interaction, infer a distribution over physical parameters and observation errors, such as camera pose and clock offset.
  2. Compile. Each sample becomes a runnable simulation world with its parameters fixed for the whole rollout.
  3. Use. The same belief predicts the outcome of a new action, warns before execution when worlds disagree, and generates training data.
