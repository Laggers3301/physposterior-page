Physics playground

Pick a new action and every world is simulated again in your browser. Blue worlds are drawn from the posterior after one observed clip; orange worlds are drawn from the prior. The dashed line is the true world.

Physics: MuJoCo 3.9.0, official WebAssembly build (@mujoco/mujoco), running locally in a Web Worker. Rendering: Three.js. Each update re-simulates 33 worlds (16 posterior, 16 prior, 1 true) with geometric primitives only. Bounce and friction-slide posteriors are joint NSF samples from our simulated benchmark; the hidden-center-of-mass posterior is illustrative.
